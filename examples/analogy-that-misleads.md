# Example: Analogy That Misleads

Topic: electric current

## Weak analogy

> Electricity is like water flowing through a pipe. The battery pumps electricity through the wire and the bulb uses it up.

## Why it is dangerous

The analogy encourages several false mappings:
- it treats “electricity” as one substance;
- it suggests the load consumes current;
- it implies charges simply travel from the battery to the bulb like water from a tank;
- it hides the role of the electric field and the closed circuit.

## Better use of the water idea

Use the analogy only for the narrow relationship it genuinely helps with, such as **rate of flow**.

```text
litres per second -> fluid flow rate
charge per second -> electric current
```

Then state the boundary:

**Where it breaks:** charge behavior in a circuit is not adequately explained by imagining a battery as a tank of electrical fluid.

The lesson should return immediately to literal electrical language.
