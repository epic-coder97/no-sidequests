# Example: Mathematical Concept

Topic: slope

### Slope measures how fast one quantity changes with another

Suppose a road rises 20 metres while you travel 100 metres horizontally.

**Key idea:** slope compares **vertical change** with **horizontal change**.

```text
vertical change     20
---------------- = ----- = 0.2
horizontal change  100
```

Only after the meaning is clear, introduce notation:

```text
slope = Δy / Δx
```

Here, `Δ` means “change in.”

**Worked example:** if height rises from 40 m to 70 m while horizontal position moves from 200 m to 350 m:

```text
Δy = 70 - 40 = 30
Δx = 350 - 200 = 150
slope = 30 / 150 = 0.2
```

**Check:** If the same 30 m rise happened over only 75 m horizontally, would the slope become larger or smaller? Explain before calculating.

## Teaching note

The prediction comes before more algebra because it checks whether the learner understands the relationship rather than merely performing subtraction and division.
