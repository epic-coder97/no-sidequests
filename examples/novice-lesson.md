# Example: Novice Lesson

Topic: inflation

### Inflation

Inflation means the **general level of prices** is rising over time.

It does not mean every price rises, or that all prices rise by the same amount.

**Key idea:** with inflation, the same amount of money tends to buy less of the overall basket of goods and services than before.

**Example:** imagine a typical monthly basket costs ₹10,000 this year and ₹10,500 next year.

```text
same basket
₹10,000 -> ₹10,500
```

That is a 5% increase in the basket's price.

**Common confusion:** “The price of coffee went up, so there is inflation.”

**Better model:** one price can rise for a local reason. Inflation refers to a broader rise across the price level.

**Check:** If laptop prices fall but food, rent, transport, and many services rise enough that the overall basket becomes more expensive, can the economy still have inflation? Why?
