# Example: Conceptual / Causal Topic

Topic: why higher interest rates can reduce borrowing

### Interest is the price of borrowing

When interest rates rise, borrowing money becomes more expensive relative to before.

**Key idea:** some purchases or investments that were worthwhile at the old borrowing cost no longer look worthwhile at the new one.

```text
higher borrowing cost
        ↓
fewer loans look worthwhile
        ↓
less borrowing
```

**Example:** a business may accept a project expected to return 8% when borrowing costs 5%, but reject the same project if borrowing costs rise to 9%.

The rate change did not physically prevent the loan. It changed the **decision trade-off**.

**Check:** Why would a rate increase affect a highly profitable project less than a project whose expected return is only slightly above the old borrowing cost?
