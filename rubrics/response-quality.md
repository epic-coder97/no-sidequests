# Response Quality Rubric

Use this rubric to evaluate a teaching response. Score each dimension from 0 to 2.

## 1. Focus

**0** — multiple unrelated learning jobs compete.  
**1** — one main job exists but side content dilutes it.  
**2** — one primary learning job is unmistakable.

## 2. Visual calm

**0** — dense wall of text or excessive formatting.  
**1** — readable but still visually busy.  
**2** — hierarchy, whitespace, and emphasis make the page easy to scan.

## 3. Mental model

**0** — mostly definitions or facts.  
**1** — some mechanism is present but implicit.  
**2** — the learner can see the central relationship or causal structure.

## 4. Language

**0** — jargon arrives before meaning.  
**1** — generally clear with some avoidable abstraction.  
**2** — plain language first; technical terms arrive when useful.

## 5. Analogy quality, if used

**0** — decorative, misleading, or unmapped.  
**1** — useful but boundaries are unclear.  
**2** — structural mapping is explicit and an important limit is stated.

If no analogy is needed, do not penalize the response.

## 6. Example quality

**0** — missing when needed, ambiguous, or atypical.  
**1** — relevant but not maximally clear.  
**2** — simple, representative, and exposes the rule.

## 7. Active learning

**0** — only exposition.  
**1** — generic question or trivial recall.  
**2** — retrieval, prediction, comparison, or transfer tests the mental model.

## 8. Adaptivity

**0** — ignores evidence of learner understanding.  
**1** — adapts level but broadly.  
**2** — responds to the precise missing relationship or next challenge.

## 9. Epistemic calibration

**0** — uncertain/current claims stated as settled.  
**1** — some caveats but unclear confidence.  
**2** — confidence matches evidence and current claims are verified when necessary.

## 10. Stopping discipline

**0** — keeps teaching after the natural boundary.  
**1** — slightly over-extended.  
**2** — stops once the current learning job is complete.

## Interpretation

- **17–20:** excellent Focus-mode response
- **13–16:** good, but simplify or sharpen one dimension
- **9–12:** likely too dense, too passive, or structurally weak
- **0–8:** redesign rather than lightly edit

A high score does not require maximum content. In this skill, restraint is a quality feature.
